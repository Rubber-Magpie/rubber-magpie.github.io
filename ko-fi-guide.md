## How to claim a copy of one of our mods on Ko-fi

This guide will show you how to download the mod for Cook, Serve, Delicious! 3?! From Rubber Magpie’s Ko-fi page. 
1.    Go to https://ko-fi.com/s/2fe74e40f5
2.    You should see a text field that says $0 or more. Enter whatever you’d like in the field—this can be 0. If you’re using a screen reader, press E to find the edit field. It should be the first one on the page. 
3.    Click either “get now” or “add to cart,” either is fine. If you’re not logged in, you will be prompted to continue as guest or sign in if you have an account. If you continue as a guest, you’ll need to provide a display name and email address. For example: hello@rubbermagpie.com, with the display name as Cricket. 
4.    Press the “check out now” button. 
5.    Here’s the tricky step. Arrow to the unlabeled dialogue and press enter. If you cannot find it, it is directly above the “next step” heading, which is at heading level 2. 
6.    Navigate to the “view content” button by pressing B. 
7.    Once “view content” is selected, arrow down until you find the second unlabeled dialogue and select it. 
8.    Select the download link, then proceed in your downloads folder. 
Enjoy!
