Hello! We’re Rubber Magpie, and we’re here to rummage through the world of games, find the shiny ones, and do our best to make them accessible to blind gamers!

## Before we get much further, let’s talk about LLMs

We can have a nuanced discussion about the ethics of LLM training and inference any day of the week—and we absolutely will if you want us to. It’s important! But for the purposes of this space, LLMs have been an invaluable tool and a gigantic step forward in the quest to make more videogames accessible to blind players.

The amount of resources spent on the creation of videogames is many orders of magnitude higher than for audiogames, and videogames are played by amounts of people many orders of magnitude higher than audiogames. Investing in equal access to the former allows us to enjoy much more similar experiences to those our sighted peers do. Apart from the loads of personal enjoyment that this brings, it also helps to significantly narrow the gap between blind and mainstream gaming, enabling us to participate in the social aspect of it in the process!

We know that not everyone feels the same way about AI, and that there are good-faith concerns about both how these systems are made and how they're used. We're not trying to sweep any of that under the rug. Those questions are incredibly important to us as well. Two of our team are writers, and we're keenly aware of the various kinds of harm that can come from irresponsible AI use. We wrestle with these questions a great deal ourselves, and that makes us all the more committed to being transparent about how and why we use these tools. With that in mind, we want to be upfront that, for Rubber Magpie, these tools have made it possible for us to tackle games and accessibility projects that would otherwise be much harder for a small team to take on.

We stand on the shoulders of giants. Even before the widespread adoption of LLMs, projects such as Pokémon Crystal Access, Stardew Access, Say the Spire and Black Hole (and a good few more!) were already bringing accessibility for the blind to major mainstream titles. With the help of coding agents, countless titles are gaining varying levels of accessibility by the week.

We’re here to do our best to add to the pool in a meaningful and thorough way.

With that out of the way, let’s get to the fun stuff!

## What will you be funding?

Primarily, LLM costs. As you will see in the below disclaimer, their reverse-engineering ability and resilience to tedious analysis work makes them a very useful tool in this space. At the time of writing, LLM subscriptions are the most effective and affordable way to access them.

Additionally, you will be investing in the time we spend in the various phases of AI-assisted mod development:

- Researching games with modding potential
- Evaluating the best ways to add accessibility with minimal change to the gameplay experience
- Prompting and supervising an initial approach
- Playtesting and thoroughly noting down bugs, inconsistencies and potential improvements
- Sanitizing and running prompts to address them until we consider the experience to be fun
- Maintaining mods after their launch, making sure the community is kept in the loop

## What will you get?

When a new mod is released, it will initially be available for purchase, giving those who choose to buy it the chance to enjoy the shiny new thing two weeks early. After those two weeks, it will become freely available to everyone.

Purchasing a mod early is simply a way to help fund the development of future mods while getting to enjoy our newest work a little sooner. If you’d rather wait, there’s absolutely no pressure to pay—just come back after two weeks and it will be waiting for you.

## AI disclaimer

The great majority of our code, and certainly the initial approach to our mods, is written by large language models. We do not claim true ownership of the code, only of the design ideas.

We believe that there is such a thing as fair use of AI and in the impact that it has had on accessibility, in so many aspects besides leisure. After many, many years of reaching out to developers with an astoundingly low response rate, we have decided to put our money and efforts into the tools that will indeed allow us to take matters into our own hands. We are aware of both the pros and the cons of this decision, and we understand that your values may differ from ours.

With all this in mind, we feel it's important to make it clear what we use AI for, and what we do not.

As we mentioned, we use AI to bridge the gap between mainstream gaming and blind gamers. We hope that accessibility will become universal enough that the use of LLMs won't be necessary, but for now, our aim is to use these tools as responsibly and ethically as we can.

However, you will never see AI in our written content, including social media posts, web content and communications with all of you. Our readmes will always be written by a member of our team.

Thanks for taking the time to rummage through our little nest. We hope you find something shiny!
